## How the rule is employed

Rule 38 exempts vessels laid down before COLREG 1972 entered into force from light and
sound-appliance specifications, some permanently and some for four or nine years. The simulation
records no keel date, and models no light ranges, colours, positions or sound appliances
(Rules 22, 33). Nothing could be exempted.

## (a)–(h)

> Any vessel (or class of vessels) provided that she complies with the requirements of the International
> Regulations for Preventing Collisions at Sea, 1960, the keel of which is laid or which is at a
> corresponding stage of construction before the entry into force of these Regulations may be exempted
> from compliance therewith as follows: (a) the installation of lights with ranges prescribed in Rule 22,
> until four years after the date of entry into force of these Regulations; (b) the installation of
> lights with colour specifications as prescribed in Section 7 of Annex I …; (c) the repositioning of
> lights as a result of conversion from Imperial to metric units …, permanent exemption; (d) the
> repositioning of masthead lights …; (e) … (f) the repositioning of sidelights …; (g) the requirements
> for sound signal appliances prescribed in Annex III …; (h) the repositioning of all-round lights …,
> permanent exemption.

**Legata.** None.

**Caveats.** The time-limited exemptions all expired by 1986. The permanent ones, (c), (d)(i)
and (h), concern light positions, which are not modelled.
